# Imagery

One real photograph and the red mark. No illustrations, icons, stock people or generated images.

## The photograph

- The hero uses "Sunrise by the Ferry Building" by Francisco Delgado (Unsplash License): palm trees beside San Francisco Bay with the Bay Bridge in morning light.
- It sits on `photo-ground` at `hero-photo` opacity (0.63) with a `bg` wash at `hero-wash` (0.24) on top, so it reads soft and warm and dark text stays legible over it.
- Crop to the waterfront: object-position 50% 36% (47% 36% on phones), 570px tall.
- Credit it in the footer ("Photo credit") and say what it is: it shows the Bay, not the event venue.

## Adding photos

- Real places and real events only, ideally our own photos from Build Night once it happens.
- Treat supporting photos like the hero: muted (about 55% saturation, 83% opacity), cropped wide, never full-bleed behind text without a wash.
- Credit every photo that isn't ours.

## Don't

- Use stock photos of people, AI-generated images, illustrations, 3D or icon sets.
- Put text directly on a busy part of a photo, or the red logo on a photo.
