# Logo

The red mark and "founder square" in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold; always the supplied SVG files, never retyped.

**Use** `logo-red.svg` on `bg`, white and `black`; `logo-white.svg` on `red`; `logo-black.svg` only for one-colour print. The mark alone (`mark-*.svg`) is for avatars and small spaces. On the site: `<img class="logo">` 29px tall in the header (22px on phones), 25px in the footer, with an empty `alt` when the link already says "Founder Square home".

**Don't** retype, capitalise, recolour, tile or shadow it, or put the red logo on red or on a photo. Full rules: Identity.
