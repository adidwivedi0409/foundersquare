# Language

We write the way a friend tells you about something worth showing up to: short, warm, specific, and honest about what isn't decided yet.

## Voice

| We are | So we write | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | "Build projects with other Berkeley students and present your work at the end of the night." | "An immersive innovation experience." |
| Welcoming | "You don't need a startup. You don't need a team." | "Open to qualified applicants." |
| Honest | "Event format and schedule will be announced soon." | Times or rooms we haven't booked |
| Direct | "Contact our team directly to discuss sponsorship." | "Reach out to learn more!" |

- Address the reader as **you**; we are **we** or "the team".
- One idea per sentence. Short paragraphs, two or three sentences.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no hype words ("innovative", "world-class", "excited to announce").

## Casing and punctuation

- Sentence case for headings and buttons: "Upcoming events", "Register by email".
- Curly apostrophes and quotes: Agent's, you're.
- The middle dot separates facts on one line: "UC Berkeley · Free · Schedule coming soon".
- Links and buttons that open something end with " ↗".
- "Coming Soon..." is the one place an ellipsis appears (the schedule panel).

## Dates, times and money

- Full: "Thursday, November 12, 2026". Short: "Thu, Nov 12, 2026" or "November 12".
- Times: 12-hour with a space and caps, "6 PM", "12:15 AM", once they're confirmed.
- Free things say "Free" or "free for Berkeley students". Don't quote prices or prizes until they're real.

## Buttons and links

- Verb first, say where it goes: "Register for Build Night ↗", "Email team@foundersquare.org ↗", "Help make the next night happen ↗".
- The copy button says "Copy address", then "Copied".

## Unconfirmed details

- Say what is known and when the rest comes: "Room shared with registrants", "Sponsor tracks and prizes will be announced when confirmed", "Socials coming soon".
- Never invent a sponsor, a judge, a room or a schedule to fill a layout.

## Examples from the site

- About: "Founder Square is a student-run community at UC Berkeley. We bring people together to turn an idea into something you can actually try."
- Build Night: "Bring your laptop, charger, and student ID. Team formation is part of the night, so coming solo is welcome."
- FAQ: "Do I need experience?" "No. There's room for people who design, code, test, write, and present."
- Sponsor: "Interested in sponsoring Agent's After Dark Build Night?"
