FAQ

Questions

Eight answers. Ask the rest at check-in, or email us.

What is Founder Square?

A student-run community of builders and founders at UC Berkeley. Build Night is our first event; the ones after it are on the events page. If you build things, or want to, you are the audience.

Who can come?

Any UC Berkeley student. Undergrad, grad, any major. You register, we confirm, you show up with a laptop.

Do I need a team?

No. Come alone and we run team formation at 7 PM. Teams are usually two to four people. If you already have one, register together.

Do I need an idea?

No. We post starter ideas at kickoff and sponsors bring problems they want solved. Picking something small enough to finish by midnight matters more than picking something clever.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Food, space, prizes and credits are covered by sponsors.

Is there food?

Yes. Dinner when doors open and a second round around 11 PM. Tell us about dietary restrictions when you register.

What if I've never built anything?

Then this is the place to start. Teams need people who can test, design, write and present, not only people who can code. Organizers and sponsor engineers float around all night to unblock you.

How are projects judged?

Each team gets three minutes in front of the panel: demo first, slides optional. Judges score on whether it works, whether anyone would use it, and how far you got in one night. Track winners are chosen by the sponsor who owns the track.
