Questions

Do I need experience?

No. There’s room for people who design, code, test, write, and present. All experience levels are welcome.

Do I need a team or idea?

Neither. You can attend on your own or bring a team. More details will be shared before the event.

What does it cost?

It’s free for Berkeley students. Dinner and late food are planned; include dietary needs in your registration email.

How are projects judged?

Demo first, slides optional. Judges consider whether it works, whether someone would use it, and how far you got in one night. Sponsor tracks and prizes will be announced when confirmed.

About

Founder Square is a student-run community at UC Berkeley. We bring people together to turn an idea into something you can actually try.

Our nights are for building, finding collaborators, and showing your work. Come with a project, a half-formed thought, or just your laptop. You don’t need a startup. You don’t need a team.
