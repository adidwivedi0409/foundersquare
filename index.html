About

Who we are

Student-run · UC Berkeley · Est. 2026

Founder Square is a student-run community at UC Berkeley for people who would rather build the thing than talk about building it. We put on nights where you build something real and show it, starting with Build Night on November 14.

Sponsors show up with credits, prizes and judges because a demo at 1 AM tells them more about a student than a resume does. You leave with a project, a few new collaborators, and the next event on your calendar.
