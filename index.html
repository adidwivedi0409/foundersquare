01 — About

Who we are

Student-run · UC Berkeley · Est. 2026 Build Night 01

A room for people who build.

Founder Square is a student-run community at UC Berkeley for people who would rather build the thing than talk about building it. Members, events, and a room on campus where it actually happens.

Our first event is Build Night. Most projects die in the planning stage, so a one-night build removes the planning stage: you start at 6 PM, and by midnight you have something that runs or you have learned why it doesn't.

Sponsors show up with credits, prizes and judges because a demo at 1 AM tells them more about a student than a resume does. You leave with a project, a few new collaborators, and the next event on your calendar.

04 — Sponsor

Sponsor the night

Credits and prizes, not cash Sponsor Build Night
The pitchFor startups

Build Night puts 100 to 150 Berkeley students in a room for one night, hands them your product, and asks them to ship something with it before midnight. You get to watch, judge, and talk to the ones you want to hire.

Tiers03
Tier 01Supporter
Supporter

Swag or credits, any amount

  • Logo on the event page and socials
  • Shoutout at kickoff
Tier 02Track
Track sponsor

Around $500 in credits or prizes

  • A named track, e.g. Best use of [Your product]
  • Time at kickoff to introduce the company
  • A seat on the judging panel
Tier 03Presenting
Presenting sponsor

Around $1,000 in credits or prizes

  • 5 minutes to present
  • A recruiting table for the night
  • A short workshop or demo
  • A seat on the judging panel
Why sponsor04 reasons
01
Students who ship

Everyone in the room chose to spend a Saturday night building. The demos at 1 AM are the filter.

02
Hiring

Judges see a student's project, code and demo before they ever see a resume. Recruiters get a table and a reason to talk.

03
Product feedback

A hundred students hitting your API for six hours will find the rough edges your docs skipped. You get the notes.

04
Cost

Credits and prizes, not cash. Most tiers are covered by things you already give away.
