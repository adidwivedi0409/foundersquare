About

Founder Square is a student-run community at UC Berkeley. We bring people together to turn an idea into something you can actually try.

Our nights are for building, finding collaborators, and showing your work. Come with a project, a half-formed thought, or just your laptop. You don’t need a startup. You don’t need a team.
