Sponsor the night

Build Night puts 100 to 150 Berkeley students in a room for one night, hands them your product, and asks them to ship something with it before midnight. You get to watch, judge, and talk to the ones you want to hire. Credits and prizes, not cash.

Why sponsor

What you get out of it

01

Students who ship

Everyone in the room chose to spend a Saturday night building. The demos at 1 AM are the filter.

02

Hiring

Judges see a student's project, code and demo before they ever see a resume. Recruiters get a table and a reason to talk.

03

Product feedback

A hundred students hitting your API all night will find the rough edges your docs skipped. You get the notes.

04

Cost

No invoice. A credits code, a prize or a box of swag counts as a full tier, and we tell you exactly what each tier needs before you commit to anything.
