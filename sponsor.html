Credits and prizes, not cashBuild Night 01 · Nov 14

Build Night puts 100 to 150 Berkeley students in a room for one night, hands them your product, and asks them to ship something with it before midnight. You get to watch, judge, and talk to the ones you want to hire.

Why sponsor04 reasons
01
Students who ship

Everyone in the room chose to spend a Saturday night building. The demos at 1 AM are the filter.

02
Hiring

Judges see a student's project, code and demo before they ever see a resume. Recruiters get a table and a reason to talk.

03
Product feedback

A hundred students hitting your API for six hours will find the rough edges your docs skipped. You get the notes.

04
Cost

Credits and prizes, not cash. Most tiers are covered by things you already give away.
